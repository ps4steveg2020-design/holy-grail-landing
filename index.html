What Holy Grail Is

Holy Grail provides two live datasets updated via automated scripts, designed for instant analysis in Excel, Google Sheets, Python, or AI tools. Here's what it offers:

  • Live player status, pricing, minutes, flags
  • Decision indicators (nailed, availability, risk)
  • No scraping. No opinions. No projections.
  • Built to be combined with your own logic for advanced FPL strategies.

How It Works

Access to Holy Grail is granted manually via email. Once subscribed, you'll receive a link to the live datasets, which are updated automatically. You can integrate the data directly into your own tools, such as Google Sheets, Python scripts, or Excel for seamless FPL analysis.
